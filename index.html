* Game Night Tournament

Smash, wager, survive the bracket.

Set up tonight's tournament

Enter one player per line. With 6 or more players, they are balanced into two pools; otherwise everyone plays in one pool.

How this runs

1. Everyone plays each other in their pool.
2. The top half enter the Winners Bracket; the rest enter the Losers Bracket.
3. Bracket winners meet in the Grand Final. The Winners Bracket champion only needs one win; the Losers Bracket champion needs two consecutive wins.
4. Before a game begins, spectators bet points and the game master starts play.

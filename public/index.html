Tournament rules

1. Pool stage

Everyone plays everyone once. The pool standings determine who starts in the winners bracket and who starts in the losers bracket.

2. Winners and losers brackets

Lose in the winners bracket and you automatically move into the next available losers-bracket round. Lose in the losers bracket and you are eliminated.

3. Grand final

The winners-bracket champion needs one grand-final win. The losers-bracket champion must win twice: winning the first set creates a reset set that decides the tournament.

4. Characters

In a randomized-character tournament, every player selects five fighters and the app randomly selects one before each match. In a regular tournament, players choose characters normally.

5. Betting

Betting is open until the game master starts the match. A bet is deducted immediately. Correct bets return double the stake; losing bets are lost.

6. Game master

The game master starts matches, confirms winners, shares the invite link, and can stop the tournament.
